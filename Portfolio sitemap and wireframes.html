Portfolio site: sitemap and wireframes

Sitemap

Shared on every page: top menu (Home, About, Skills, Gallery, Contact) and a footer with the same links.
The visitor's path: Home: who is this?→ About + Skills: can they do it?→ Gallery: show me→ Contact: let's talk

1. Home

index.html: the first impression.

index.html
← Menu bar
Intro / hero
← Portrait or logo
← Your name
← Job title
Vision
← Vision statement
Qualifications
← Four certificate cards
Technology icons
← Tool icons
← Footer links
What it's forSay who you are in a few seconds, then point people toward the rest of the portfolio.
IntroA portrait or logo sits beside your name and what you do.
Vision and qualificationsOne short line about where you're headed, backed by four certificates.
Next step for visitorsUse the top menu to explore.

About.html

What it's forBuild trust. Show where you've learned and what you've built.
TimelineFour certificates stacked in order, each with a line or two of context.
Projects listFour project names in a slim column beside the timeline.
On a phoneEverything stacks into a single column.
